# Holiday Gift Baskets page: open questions for Liberty Oak

The page is built from the Holiday Gifting 2026 PDF. These answers decide what else goes live.

## Ordering and delivery
1. **Order deadline:** what is the last day to order for delivery before Christmas? Is there also a cut-off for New Year?
2. **Delivery area:** the PDF lists rates up to 30 km. Can we write "delivered across Toronto", or should the page say "within 30 km of Exhibition Place"? Is delivery beyond 30 km possible on request?
3. **Delivery days:** which days do you deliver (Monday to Friday? weekends?), and do customers get a time window?
4. **Pickup:** can customers pick up at Exhibition Place instead of paying for delivery?
5. **Payment:** how do customers pay (credit card by phone, payment link, invoice for companies)? When is payment taken?

## Gifts
6. **Gift card or message:** does every basket include a card with a personal message? Is it free?
7. **Corporate branding:** for company orders, can you add a logo label, branded card or ribbon? Is there a minimum quantity or extra cost?
8. **Volume orders:** is there a discount or a different process for larger corporate orders, for example 10+ baskets?
9. **Alcohol:** must the recipient be 19+ and show ID at delivery? What happens if nobody of age is home?

## Order form on the page
10. **Where should online orders go?** Choose one:
    - (a) An email to melissa@libertygroup.com (we set up a free Resend account for this)
    - (b) A Formspree form
    - (c) A separate Tripleseat lead form for gift orders (please share the form ID)
11. Orders still go to melissa@libertygroup.com, although the page now says "Liberty Oak Catering Team". Should they go to a general address instead, and should anyone receive a copy?
